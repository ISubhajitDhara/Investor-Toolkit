About Investor Toolkit

Investor Toolkit is an independent financial education project designed to make everyday money concepts easier to understand in English and Bengali.

What you can learn

Explore beginner lessons and guides about insurance, investing, banking, saving and financial planning. Use quizzes to check your understanding and save lessons in your browser.

Our approach

We aim to explain concepts in accessible language, distinguish education from recommendations and encourage readers to verify current product terms with official sources.
